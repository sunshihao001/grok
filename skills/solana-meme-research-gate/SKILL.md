2. Separate **structural identification** from **incremental prediction** and **executable net trade capture**. Treat all three as separate experiments.
3. Write the study-specific sample frame, independent unit, competing hypotheses, explicit falsifier, baseline and proper null, timestamp availability, sample attrition, multiple testing rule, and exit/fill/fee convention **before** reading outcomes.
